What is Zero Trust?

Posted on 30 Sep 2026 by Ethan Vu

Perimeter-based network security is no longer sufficient in distributed cloud environments. Zero Trust operates on the principle of "never trust, always verify" — enforcing continuous identity verification, least-privilege access, and micro-segmentation.
